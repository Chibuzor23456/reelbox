## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** Realbox  
**Product Category:** Digital TV, Live Streaming & On-Demand Entertainment Platform

Realbox is a hybrid streaming platform combining a cable-TV-style live viewing experience with a standalone on-demand library for movies and series.

The brand should feel like a modern digital entertainment product rather than a traditional IPTV service.

### Brand Personality

- Cinematic
- Premium
- Modern
- Bold
- Minimal
- Confident
- Accessible
- Technology-driven

### Brand Principle

Realbox should feel like **your own digital entertainment box** — a place where live television and on-demand entertainment come together in one simple experience.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

Realbox is **not positioned as a Netflix clone**.

Netflix may be used as a UX reference for premium streaming conventions, but Realbox must establish its own visual identity.

### Product Identity

**REALBOX**

The name combines:

- **REAL** — real entertainment, real-time viewing, real television.
- **BOX** — a digital entertainment box containing channels, movies, series and other content.

The visual identity should communicate both **live television** and **on-demand entertainment**.

---

## 3. Core Visual Direction

The Realbox visual language must combine:

- Dark cinematic environments.
- Strong red brand accents.
- High-contrast white typography.
- Generous visual breathing room.
- Large content artwork.
- Clean modern interface components.
- Subtle motion.
- Clear content hierarchy.

The interface should feel premium and immersive without becoming visually heavy.

### Avoid

- Generic IPTV dashboard aesthetics.
- Dense tables in the consumer experience.
- Excessive red backgrounds.
- Excessive gradients.
- Cheap-looking neon effects.
- Overly decorative UI.
- Copying Netflix's exact visual identity.
- Random colors outside the approved system.

---

## 5. Color Usage Rules

Red must be treated as an **accent**, not the dominant background color.

```text
Midnight   #080808  → Main background
Surface    #121212  → Navigation / sections
Card       #1B1B1B  → Content cards
White      #FFFFFF  → Primary text
Soft Gray  #A1A1A1  → Secondary text
Border     #282828  → Dividers / boundaries
Red        #E31B23  → Brand / actions / active states
Deep Red   #A80F18  → Deeper brand states
```

A typical Realbox screen should be predominantly dark neutral tones, with Realbox Red appearing strategically to direct attention.

Do not make entire pages red unless a specific campaign or branded marketing treatment requires it.

## 6. Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Manrope**

Manrope is the approved primary typeface for Realbox V1.

### Approved Weights

- 400 — Regular
- 500 — Medium
- 600 — Semibold
- 700 — Bold
- 800 — Extra Bold

No additional primary typeface should be introduced without explicit brand approval.

### Typography Hierarchy

The system should define:

- Display
- H1
- H2
- H3
- H4
- Body
- Body Small
- Caption
- Metadata
- Labels
- Buttons
- Navigation
- Content badges

Realbox typography should feel clean, modern, confident, highly readable and spacious for major headings.

## 7. Logo System

Realbox requires a complete logo family.

### Required Logo Assets

- Primary logo — icon/mark + REALBOX wordmark.
- Secondary/horizontal logo.
- Stacked logo.
- Icon/mark only.
- Wordmark only.

### Color Variations

- Full-color version.
- Red-on-dark version.
- White/reversed version.
- Black/monochrome version.
- Red monochrome version where appropriate.

The icon should remain recognizable when reduced to small digital sizes.

---

## 8. Logo Usage

The logo must maintain clear space around the mark and wordmark.

Do not:

- Stretch the logo.
- Compress the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Add arbitrary shadows.
- Add unapproved gradients.
- Change approved colors.
- Place the logo on backgrounds where contrast is insufficient.
- Recreate the logo using substitute fonts.

---

## 9. App Icon & Favicon

The Realbox icon/mark must work independently of the wordmark.

Required assets:

- Favicon.
- Browser icon.
- 192×192 PWA icon.
- 512×512 PWA icon.
- Maskable PWA icon.
- Apple touch icon.
- App launcher icon.
- Social avatar/profile icon.

The icon should remain recognizable at small sizes and should not depend on tiny text.

Content cards are a major part of the Realbox identity.

### Movie Card

May display:

- Poster.
- Title.
- Year.
- Genre.
- Runtime.
- Rating where available.

### Series Card

May display:

- Poster.
- Series title.
- Number of seasons.
- Genre.
- Current episode information where relevant.

### Live Channel Card

May display:

- Channel logo.
- Channel name.
- Current programme.
- LIVE indicator.
- Country/region.
- Category.

### Continue Watching Card

May display:

- Artwork.
- Title.
- Season/episode.
- Progress bar.
- Remaining time where available.

### Card Principles

- Content artwork should remain the visual focus.
- Metadata should be concise.
- Hover/focus states should be clear.
- Red should be used for actions and progress rather than covering the card.
- Cards should feel premium and lightweight.

## 13. UI Design Tokens

The Realbox design system must define reusable tokens for:

### Color

- Background.
- Surface.
- Card.
- Text.
- Muted text.
- Border.
- Primary.
- Primary-hover.
- Primary-active.
- Error.
- Success.
- Warning.

### Spacing

Use a consistent spacing scale across navigation, sections, cards, forms, modals and player controls.

### Radius

Define consistent corner-radius levels for cards, buttons, inputs, modals and badges.

### Elevation

Use subtle elevation only where it improves hierarchy. Avoid heavy shadows.

### Motion

Define consistent:

- Hover transitions.
- Page transitions.
- Card interactions.
- Modal transitions.
- Player state transitions.
- Loading states.

Motion should feel smooth and restrained.

## 20. Accessibility

Brand implementation must maintain accessible contrast and interaction states.

Requirements include:

- Sufficient text contrast.
- Visible keyboard focus states.
- Clear active states.
- Do not use color as the only indicator of meaning.
- Red live indicators should be accompanied by text or another accessible indicator.
- Interactive controls should be clearly identifiable.
- Typography must remain readable across mobile and desktop.

---

## 21. Brand Do's

- Use Realbox Red strategically.
- Maintain the dark cinematic environment.
- Keep typography clean and consistent.
- Prioritize content artwork.
- Maintain generous spacing.
- Use consistent iconography.
- Use subtle motion.
- Keep UI modern and uncluttered.
- Maintain strong contrast.
- Make Realbox recognizable even without the wordmark.

---

## 22. Brand Don'ts

- Do not copy Netflix's logo or exact visual identity.
- Do not use random red shades.
- Do not make the entire interface red.
- Do not introduce unrelated fonts.
- Do not use excessive gradients.
- Do not use excessive glow/neon effects.
- Do not make the consumer UI look like a traditional IPTV control panel.
- Do not overcrowd content cards.
- Do not mix incompatible icon styles.
- Do not sacrifice readability for visual effects.

## 25. Final Brand Direction

Realbox should be recognized through a combination of:

**Dark cinematic interface + Realbox Red + Manrope + strong content artwork + minimal modern UI + restrained motion.**

The goal is not to look like an existing streaming platform with a different name.

The goal is to establish **Realbox as its own digital entertainment brand** while retaining the familiarity and usability users expect from modern streaming products.
